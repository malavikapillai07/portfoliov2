A Little About Me

My journey into design started at a very young age, quite literally on the walls of my home. What began as childhood scribbles was encouraged by my parents and slowly grew into a love for drawing, creating, and exploring ideas. (I still paint on walls, by the way!)

Over the years, that curiosity found its way into communication design and eventually product design. I enjoy understanding people, solving problems, and creating experiences that make a difference.

Beyond design, I find joy in dancing, getting lost in books, and discovering inspiration in unexpected places.
