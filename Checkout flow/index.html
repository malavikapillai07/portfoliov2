Whether you're placing orders or sending gifts, doing it for people in different places means repeating the same steps again and again. Add the item, enter an address, pay. Then start over for the next person, and the next. Each round brings another payment, another delivery charge, and another chance to mix up an address. It's not hard, just tiring, and far more effort than it should be.

“What if one cart could send items to different people, with one payment?”
